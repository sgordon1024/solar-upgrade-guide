Your route

Check marks mean preparation done. A lock means preview only.

Before tools come out

The four-panel plan is selected. The mounting and electrical details are not approved. No button in this guide releases installation holds.

Evidence still needed

  • Rack model; rail slots, crossbars, brackets and shared attachments; approved fasteners and torque; roof/rack dynamic and wind-uplift limits.
  • Driver-side Starlink and centerline exhaust-fan footprints/separation; panel 2 placement; fan fully open and A/C measured from one agreed rail datum; required airflow and service clearances; validated panel support and overhang.
  • Both disconnects: DC voltage/current ratings, poles, topology and load-break suitability.
  • Actual cable markings, condition and protection; connector manufacturer/family; measured jumper routes; bonding design.
  • Qualified isolation plan for all affected energy sources; manufacturer removal and mounting instructions; safe access and lifting plan.
  • Cold-weather string-voltage calculation, polarity and protection checks; battery/BMS settings and commissioning procedure.

Official references

  • HQST HSP200N-64L specification PDF — dimensions and electrical data. Despite its filename, this is a two-page datasheet, not detailed mounting instructions.
  • Victron safety precautions — hazardous DC and isolation.
  • Victron installation manual — array limits and system-specific installation requirements.
  • Mesa Riser installation guide — no panel wind-uplift rating. Mesa is not an approved mount for this plan.

Sources reviewed 1 November 2026. Rack, existing-panel, A/C, fan and battery-specific instructions remain to be collected. A revised, verified guide is needed before work begins.
