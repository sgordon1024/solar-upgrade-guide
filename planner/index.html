              Dimensions, sources & electrical differences

              Mono: 46.26 × 30.12 × 1.18 in, 20.17 lb. Bifacial: 47.24 × 30.12 × 1.18 in, 21.58 lb. Geometry uses manufacturer millimeters. The extra 0.984 in can consume nearly all of a 1-inch row gap.

              Both datasheets list 200 W, 22.4 V Voc, 19.2 V Vmp, 10.5 A Imp, 20 A maximum series fuse, −0.28%/°C Voc and +0.048%/°C Isc coefficients. Bifacial Isc: 11.4 A versus mono 11.04 A. Pmax coefficients: −0.29%/°C bifacial and −0.37%/°C mono. These are front-side reference values, not a controller approval.
